Privacy Policy for Arthur

Last updated: 2026-10-15

What Arthur is

Arthur is a personal AI assistant app used by a single owner to read and manage their own Google account data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive and Contacts). It is not offered to the public.

What data Arthur accesses

Arthur accesses only the owner's own Google account data, through official Google APIs, and only with the owner's explicit OAuth consent. It is used solely for the owner's personal automation, such as reading and organizing email, managing calendar events, and finding files and contacts.

Where data is stored

Data retrieved from Google APIs is processed on and stays on the owner's own computer. Arthur does not operate a server that collects or stores user data.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data is not used to build user profiles for any purpose other than providing the assistant's features to the owner.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Arthur's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke Arthur's access to your Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
